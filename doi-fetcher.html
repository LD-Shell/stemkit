How-to guide

How to turn DOIs into BibTeX

Paste one DOI or hundreds and the tool looks each one up at doi.org, adding a BibTeX entry for it to a bibliography you can copy or save as a .bib file.

1

Paste your DOIs

One per line, or separated by commas, semicolons or spaces. Bare DOIs and doi.org links both work. A repeated DOI, or one already in the bibliography, is skipped. Load example adds three to try.

2

Check the separator

Auto-detect handles most lists. If it splits yours wrongly, pick New line, Comma, Semicolon or Space.

3

Fetch

Press Fetch BibTeX. Entries arrive one at a time with a progress bar; any that fail are listed with the reason, and Retry failed tries them again.

4

Choose fields and save

Untick the fields you do not want in step 2, then Copy the bibliography or Save .bib to download references.bib.

How resolution works

DOI content negotiation

Each DOI is requested from doi.org with an Accept: application/x-bibtex header. The DOI system routes to the registering agency (Crossref, DataCite, mEDRA) and returns BibTeX, the same mechanism reference managers use.

Batching, repeats & retry

A DOI repeated in the list, or already fetched since you last cleared the bibliography, is skipped. The rest are fetched one after another with a short pause between requests, and any failures are collected into a report you can retry in one click. Unticked fields are removed in your browser from the returned entries.

Privacy note: formatting and filtering happen in your browser, but resolving a DOI necessarily sends it to doi.org to retrieve the metadata, that network step is how DOI resolution works. No account or tracking is involved.

Frequently asked questions

Does this use a private server?
No STEMKit server is involved. Your browser contacts the public doi.org content-negotiation service directly to resolve each DOI.
Which DOIs are supported?
Any DOI whose registration agency exposes BibTeX via content negotiation, this covers the great majority of Crossref (journal/conference) and many DataCite (dataset) DOIs.
Why did some DOIs fail?
Common causes are a typo, a DOI whose agency doesn't return BibTeX, or a transient network error. Failed DOIs are listed and can be retried in one click.
Can I remove fields like abstract or url?
Yes. Untick a field in step 2 and it is removed from every entry, in your browser. Fields the panel does not list, such as address or school, are kept.
Are duplicates handled?
Yes. A DOI repeated in the list, or already fetched since the bibliography was last cleared, is skipped rather than fetched again, and the count is reported. Two different DOIs for the same work are not detected; the BibTeX Deduplicator can find those by title.
