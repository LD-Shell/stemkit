Legal & Privacy

Because STEMKit's tools run in your browser, our policies are simple: your data is your business.

Privacy Policy

1. Your data stays in your browser

STEMKit runs on your computer. Whether you are cleaning a CSV, inspecting a structure, running an ANOVA or tidying BibTeX, every calculation happens in JavaScript in your browser, and the files you open are read there. We do not upload, receive or store the data, files or text you work with. Two features contact another service, and only when you use them: DOI to BibTeX sends the DOIs you enter to doi.org, which passes each lookup to the agency that registered the DOI (Crossref, DataCite or another), and the Structure Inspector sends a four-character PDB ID to RCSB when you ask it to fetch that entry. Those services receive the request as any website would, including your IP address. Nothing else you enter is sent.

2. Local storage and cookies

We do not use cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. Some tools use your browser's localStorage to remember things between visits: the light or dark theme, dismissed hints, panel sizes and open tabs, the Structure Inspector's spin speed, timer and script-generator settings, and the custom journal abbreviations you add. It stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

3. Hosting

STEMKit is a static website. Every script, stylesheet, font and icon is served from the site itself; no page loads anything from another host. Like any website, our hosting provider may log standard connection data (such as IP address, time and browser user agent) for security and routing. Those logs cannot contain the data you process in the tools, because that data never leaves your browser.

4. Personal data (GDPR, CCPA)

STEMKit has no accounts and keeps no record of what you do in the tools, and it does not collect, store or process the data you work with. The only personal data involved is connection data: the IP address and browser details that the host may log (section 3), and that doi.org or RCSB receive if you use those two features (section 1). The data you analyse stays under your control.
